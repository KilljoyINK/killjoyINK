01 Who We Are

WHO
WE
ARE

Ink Labs is an interdisciplinary research group embedded within a top-tier engineering school. We partner with industry and government agencies to tackle engineering challenges that sit at the intersection of hardware, software, and intelligent systems.

Our work spans full project lifecycles — from first-principles concept development and rapid prototyping through to rigorous validation and technology transfer. We believe the most compelling engineering happens when domain expertise collides with fresh perspective.

02 Mission & Values

OUR
MISSION

We exist to close the gap between cutting-edge research and deployable engineering solutions. Every project we take on must push a technical boundary, contribute to the open knowledge base, and develop the next generation of engineers in the process.

We value rigour over speed, transparency in method, and collaboration over competition. Our lab doors are open.
